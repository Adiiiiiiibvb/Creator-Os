FACT #1 · 0:40–1:30

FACT #2 · 1:30–2:20
Time slows down near a black hole. Einstein predicted this with general relativity, and it's real. If you could hover just outside the event horizon for what feels like an hour to you, thousands of years would pass on Earth. This isn't science fiction — it's the same effect that makes GPS satellites have to correct their clocks daily.
